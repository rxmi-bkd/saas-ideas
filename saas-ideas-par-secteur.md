# SaaS ideas by sector (validated categories, social-media friendly)

Each idea is a product category that already has paying customers, with real competitors named as proof. The strategy is to build a simpler, cheaper, niche version for one underserved audience.

Note: this comes from my knowledge, not live checks. Verify pricing and traction before committing.

**Target math:** €2,500 MRR is about 50 customers at €50/mo, or about 85 at €29/mo.

## Top picks for the €2,500 MRR goal
These combine a B2B price point, a large audience of small professionals, and content that makes good short videos.
1. **Invoicing and URSSAF for micro-entrepreneurs** (9.1). Huge audience, and finance explainers perform well.
2. **Rental management for landlords** (4.1). Landlord law and tax content is popular.
3. **Quotes for tradespeople** (3.1). Tradespeople watch a lot of short video, and the educational angle (how to price a job) is also the product pitch.
4. **AI clipping for shorts** (10.2). It's a tool for the exact audience you'd be posting to, so you use your own product as marketing.
5. **Flashcards and quiz AI** (11.3). Students are very active on TikTok, but consumer pricing makes €2.5k harder, so charge a premium tier.
